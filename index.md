**Philip Moore** is a pianist, chamber musician and Steinway Artist whose work ranges from concerto and two-piano repertoire to ensemble playing and orchestral piano.

Originally from the Vale of Evesham, Philip studied at London's Royal Academy of Music with Hamish Milne, later receiving the Meaker Fellowship and becoming an Associate of the RAM.

Much of Philip's musical life has been shaped by collaboration. His long-standing piano duo with Simon Crawford-Phillips has won international prizes and a Borletti-Buitoni Trust Fellowship, and has given major premieres by Detlev Glanert, Anna Meredith and Steve Reich. Their performances of Reich's *Quartet*, with Colin Currie and Sam Walton, have taken them to Carnegie Hall, Tokyo Opera City, Kölner Philharmonie, Cité de la musique Paris and other international venues. Their Nonesuch recording was named one of *The Guardian*'s Top 10 classical releases of 2018.

Philip is a member of the Colin Currie Group, the former pianist of Hebrides Ensemble, and a regular guest with London Sinfonietta, the Nash Ensemble and Chamber Orchestra of Europe. He has performed and broadcast throughout Europe, America and the Far East.

Alongside his chamber and duo work, Philip is in demand as an orchestral pianist, appearing with all of the London symphony orchestras. In 2017 he joined the London Symphony Orchestra for Sir Simon Rattle's inaugural concerts as Music Director, performing *Petrushka* in London and Paris.
